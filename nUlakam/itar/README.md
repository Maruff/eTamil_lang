# itar — இடர்

Risk, and estimating under uncertainty.

A risk register with probability, impact and expected value, signed by whether the risk is a threat or an opportunity; responses and whether they are worth their cost; and the contingency and management reserves that follow from the register.

`mUZRuppuLLi.qmz` is three-point estimation. Its most useful function is `தவறாக_விலகல்களைக்_கூட்டினால்`, which exists to show what happens when standard deviations are added instead of variances — a mistake common enough to be worth demonstrating rather than describing.
