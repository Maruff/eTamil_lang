# varuvAy — வருவாய்

Revenue recognition, in the five steps the standard sets out.

Is there a contract; what are the performance obligations; what is the transaction price; how is it allocated; and when is each obligation satisfied. Progress can be measured by cost or by output, and `muZZERRam.qmz` keeps them separate because they give different answers.

Also here: contract modifications and the three ways they can be accounted for, principal versus agent, contract costs and onerous contracts, and the contract asset or liability that ends up on the balance sheet.
